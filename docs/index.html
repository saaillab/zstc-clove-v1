Deep Learning Feasibility Study for Automated Clove Quality Classification Using CNN Architectures on a Novel Zanzibar Dataset

Patrick Vincent Ndowo 1 , Innocent Nyalala 1,2
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania
2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
4th EAC STI Conference / AI for East Africa Conference (AI4EAC 2026), Kigali, Rwanda (Mar 2026)
Code Dataset / Split Poster BibTeX

Summary: Empirical CNN feasibility benchmark on ZSTC-Clove-V1 (5,298 single-clove images across four ZSTC grades): ResNet-18 achieves 94.46% test accuracy (94.33% macro-F1), whereas plain VGG-16 collapses to 31.32% majority-class prediction.

Abstract

We investigate the technical feasibility of automated clove quality grading using deep learning on ZSTC-Clove-V1, a novel dataset of 5,298 single-clove images across four official commercial grades collected at the Zanzibar State Trading Corporation (Saateni warehouse, Unguja). ResNet-18 achieves 94.46% accuracy (94.33% F1), whereas VGG-16 fails to converge at 31.32%, demonstrating sharp architectural sensitivity on fine-grained agricultural commodities and underscoring the need for trustworthy deployment.

  1. Constructed ZSTC-Clove-V1, a 5,298-image single-clove benchmark graded by certified Zanzibar State Trading Corporation (ZSTC) inspectors (Grade I: 1,455; Grade II: 1,320; Grade III: 1,398; Grade IV: 1,125) under 70/15/15 stratified splits (kappa = 0.87).
  2. Empirically demonstrated dramatic architectural divergence on fine-grained spice surfaces: residual connections enable ResNet-18 to reach 94.46% accuracy and 94.39% precision, whereas plain VGG-16 fails to converge (31.32% accuracy, 14.94% F1).
  3. Analyzed spatial Grad-CAM attributions showing why pure black box classifiers risk latching onto background tray cues rather than botanical bud discoloration (mpeta), directly motivating subsequent segmentation-first grading systems.
