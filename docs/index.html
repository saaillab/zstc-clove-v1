Deep Learning Feasibility Study for Automated Clove Quality Classification Using CNN Architectures on a Novel Zanzibar Dataset

Patrick Vincent Ndowo 1 , Innocent Nyalala 1,2
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania · 2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
4th EAC STI Conference / AI for East Africa Conference (AI4EAC 2026), Kigali, Rwanda (Mar 2026)
Code Dataset / Split BibTeX

TL;DR: Presentation at the 4th EAC STI / AI4EAC Conference in Kigali, Rwanda evaluating automated clove grading across 5,298 ZSTC specimens.

Abstract

We investigate the technical feasibility of automated clove quality grading using deep learning on ZSTC-Clove-V1, a novel dataset of 5,298 single-clove images across four official commercial grades collected at the Zanzibar State Trading Corporation (Saateni warehouse, Unguja). ResNet-18 achieves 94.46% accuracy (94.33% F1), whereas VGG-16 fails to converge at 31.32%, demonstrating sharp architectural sensitivity on fine-grained agricultural commodities and underscoring the need for trustworthy deployment.
